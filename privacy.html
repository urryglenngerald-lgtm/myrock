myRock privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

myRock (the website myrockmalta.com and the myRock Android app) is run by Glenn Gerald Urry, trading as Gerald's Media House, Malta. Contact: geraldsmediahouse@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. myRock has no accounts, no sign up, no tracking and no analytics. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

What stays on your phone

Events you save with the bookmark button are kept only on your own device (in the browser's local storage). We never see them. Clearing the app's data removes them.

Other services

To show the weather, the app asks Open-Meteo for the current weather in Malta. It does not send your location. Links to events, ferries, pharmacies and ride apps open the organiser's or operator's own website or app, which have their own privacy policies.

Ads and sponsored listings

Some events and cards are marked Featured or Sponsored because a venue or promoter paid for the spot. These are not personalised and use no data about you.

Children

myRock is meant for people aged 18 and over, because many listings are nightlife events.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
